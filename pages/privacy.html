Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Wargio Anna Edition ("Wargio", "we", "our") is an AI business assistant for Indonesian warung (micro-retail) owners, distributed as an App on the Anna platform.

1. Data We Process

Wargio processes the following types of data through its tools:

  • Inventory data — product names, stock quantities, and restock thresholds
  • Sales data — transaction amounts, dates, product sold
  • Debt records — customer names and outstanding balances
  • Payment records — payment amounts and customer references

In the current version, all data is synthetic/demo data stored in a dedicated demo database. No real business or personal data is collected.

2. Data Storage

  • Business data is stored in an external MongoDB Atlas database (demo cluster).
  • Draft/pending operations are stored in process memory during your session and are not persisted after the session ends.
  • We do not store conversation history — that is managed by the Anna platform according to their privacy policy.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information (KTP, email, phone).
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • We do not store your chat messages or conversation logs.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anna Platform — provides the runtime environment, agent, and user authentication. See Anna's Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas — hosts the demo business database on a free-tier cluster (region: Southeast Asia).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Demo data is retained indefinitely for functionality. When production multi-tenant support is added, users will be able to request deletion of their business data by contacting us.

6. Security

  • Database connections use TLS encryption.
  • Database credentials are stored as Executa developer credentials (not in source code).
  • The demo database is isolated from any production systems.

7. Children's Privacy

Wargio is a business tool intended for adult warung owners. We do not knowingly process data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features evolve (particularly when adding real multi-tenant data). Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

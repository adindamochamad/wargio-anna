Available Tools

Wargio provides these tools via natural language chat:

  • get_inventory — Check stock levels, find products needing restock
  • get_sales — View revenue for today or this week
  • get_debts — Check a customer's debt or list all debtors
  • record_payment — Record a customer payment (with confirmation)
  • record_sale — Record a sale, cash or credit (with confirmation)
  • sales_forecast — Predict tomorrow's business level

Example Queries

Try these in your Anna chat with #wargio:

  • "Produk apa yang hampir habis?" — Check low stock
  • "Berapa stok Indomie?" — Check specific product
  • "Berapa penjualan minggu ini?" — Weekly sales
  • "Siapa yang masih punya hutang?" — List debtors
  • "Catat pembayaran Budi 50000" — Record payment
  • "Prediksi besok ramai atau sepi?" — Sales forecast

FAQ

Is my data real or demo?
The current version uses synthetic demo data. No real business data is collected or stored. This is intentional for the beta period.
Can I use Wargio for my actual warung?
Not yet. Multi-tenant support with real data isolation is planned for a future release. For now, it demonstrates the AI assistant workflow with demo data.
Why does Wargio ask for confirmation before writing?
Write operations (record_payment, record_sale) always show a summary and require you to say "ya" or "yes" before committing. This prevents accidental data changes.
What language does Wargio speak?
Bilingual — Bahasa Indonesia and English. Wargio responds in whichever language you use.
The tool returned no data / error
The demo database has a limited dataset. If you query something outside the demo fixtures, you may get empty results. This is expected.
